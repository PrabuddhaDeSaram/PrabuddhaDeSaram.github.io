**Project type:** Final-year Research & Development project, University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka  
**Advisor:** Prof. Y.W.R. Amarasinghe

This project investigated an automated planting approach intended to address mechanisation challenges associated with paddy transplanting in Sri Lanka, particularly for short-term paddy varieties where conventional dapog-type transplanters may not be suitable.

### Engineering contribution

- Developed the mechanical concept and overall transplanting system.
- Optimised the Venturi-based transplanting mechanism using **CFD analysis and practical experiments**.
- Applied mechanical system design, multibody dynamics, fabrication, assembly, and field testing during development.
- The final prototype achieved a reported **92.8% successful transplanting rate** during testing.
- The work subsequently resulted in a peer-reviewed engineering conference publication.
