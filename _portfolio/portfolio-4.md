**Project type:** Self-initiated individual engineering project

This project involved the design, analysis, fabrication, assembly, and testing of a column-type 4-axis CNC milling machine intended for practical metal machining.

### Engineering scope

- Designed and fabricated a CNC milling platform with an approximately **300 mm × 300 mm × 200 mm** working envelope.
- Integrated mechanical structure, linear-motion components, drive systems, spindle arrangement, and machine controls into a functional prototype.
- Applied practical design-for-manufacture and machining considerations throughout fabrication and assembly.
- The completed system demonstrated machining of mild steel with a reported tested tolerance of approximately **0.02 mm**.
