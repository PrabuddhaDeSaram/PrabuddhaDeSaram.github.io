## Mechanical Engineer | Advanced Manufacturing | Research & Development

I am a mechanical engineer and final-stage PhD researcher with experience across conventional and advanced manufacturing, engineering design, and multidisciplinary research and development. My background spans practical workshop engineering and machining operations through to microfabrication, multiphysics modelling, experimental characterisation, and data analysis.

I previously worked as a Service Engineer in the Engine Care Unit of Diesel & Motor Engineering PLC (DIMO), where I was involved in the technical operation of a large engine machining and rebuilding workshop. My responsibilities included machining-process planning, tooling and spare-parts planning, technical troubleshooting, workshop safety, machine maintenance, and maintaining workshop requirements associated with ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.

I am currently completing a PhD in Mechanical & Industrial Engineering at Griffith University. My research focuses on developing micropillar-based platforms that enable spatial control of cellular microenvironment stiffness, combining mechanical design, advanced microfabrication, experimental characterisation, multiphysics modelling, and quantitative analysis. My fabrication and research activities have included work through the Australian National Fabrication Facility (ANFF).

### Areas of experience

- **Manufacturing & workshop operations:** conventional machining, tooling and process planning, process optimisation, workshop operations, troubleshooting, quality, environmental and safety practices.
- **Mechanical design & engineering:** CAD, design for manufacture, prototyping, fabrication, SolidWorks, and engineering analysis.
- **Advanced manufacturing & microfabrication:** femtosecond laser micromachining, two-photon polymerisation, maskless lithography, soft lithography, PDMS microfabrication, and reactive ion etching.
- **Modelling & data analysis:** COMSOL Multiphysics, Python, image processing, quantitative experimental analysis, and engineering data analysis.
- **Biomedical research:** mammalian cell culture, cell mechanobiology, cellular morphology and migration analysis, fluorescence-based cellular assays, and general biological laboratory techniques.

This website presents selected engineering projects, research activities, publications, and professional experience. Additional projects will be added as the portfolio develops.
