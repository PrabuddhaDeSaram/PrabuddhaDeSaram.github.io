## Current Research Direction

My current research uses height-encoded micropillar platforms to engineer spatial variations in cellular microenvironment stiffness and investigate how cells respond to mechanical heterogeneity at cell and multicellular spatial scales. The work integrates mechanical design, microfabrication, mechanical characterisation, cell experiments, microscopy, image analysis, and quantitative modelling.
